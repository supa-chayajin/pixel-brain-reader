**Status:** reviewed by the owner in an interview on 2026-11-10 (gate 1: D1 and D3 touch the
burn). The owner's earlier decisions it follows: the rollover only offers and never touches

## D3 — Health section in the burned note

- **What.** Each burn ends the day's note with:

  ```markdown
  ## 🩺 Health

  - Sleep: 7 h 12 min
  - Sleep stages: deep 1 h 05 min · REM 1 h 30 min · light 4 h 10 min
  - Steps: 8,421
  - HRV: 42 ms
  - Resting heart rate: 57 bpm
  - Recovery: in your usual range
  ```

  Only measured values appear (0 = not measured, so that line is left out). The stages line comes
  when the watch recorded stages, and the recovery line once the 4-week baseline gives a verdict
  (the owner added both in the review). When there is nothing to say, there is no section. The
  sources are the day's health file (`10_Journal/data/health/metrics/<date>.json`) and Life Stats'
  recovery readout (Health Connect when it answers, else the vault's health files), read at each
  burn (`DailyHealthSection`).
- **Round trip (#7).** The heading is matched whole, emoji included. A user's own `## Health`
  inside Notes stays theirs. The parser skips the lines the section writes (strict shapes) and
  nothing enters Room. Any other line found under the heading (a line added at the end of the note
  on the desktop) moves into Notes instead of being dropped. Tests: burn → parse → burn is
  byte-identical with the section; the section is rewritten, never stacked; the board is the same
  with or without it.
- **Pushes (kept in the review).** A burn whose only change is the health lines requests **no push**, like
  the health file itself (`SyncHealthDataUseCase` never requests one). The lines go up with the
  next commit. Without this rule, leaving the app after each 5-minute health refresh would add a
  commit to the vault.
- **Search.** Today's note stays unembedded (#3), so a day's health becomes searchable the next
  day.

## D1 — Task rollover, offered

- **Source.** The most recent day before today that has a note or a board, at most 7 days back.
  That way a weekend without the app still offers Friday. The day's burned note is read first: it
  is the day's last word, desktop ticks included. Room is the fallback.
- **Offer.** The day's unfinished local tasks. Left out: Google tasks (the Google import keeps
  those), a task today's board already holds (same label, whatever its case or spacing), and a
  task already handled on this phone.
- **Bring** adds the task to today's board with its priority and without its old time.
  **Dismiss** forgets it. Both are remembered on the phone only (DataStore `rollover_handled`,
  `yyyy-MM-dd|label` keys, dropped after 7 days). Bring all and Dismiss all appear when there is
  more than one task.
- **Never written:** the earlier day's note and any file. Bring writes today's board, which the
  next burn carries as usual, so the burn format doesn't change.
- **Where.** A card under the banners on today's page only, shown only when there is something to
  offer. Never on a read-only device.

## D2 — "Due today" block, and the recovery verdict

- **Block** `due` (a persisted `DailySection` key, never renamed). It is added by the update, so it
  arrives in "More today" at the end of a saved order, until the owner places it.
- **Lines:**
  - objective milestones that are open and due today or earlier;
  - `@moi` actions that are open and due today or earlier, from every note but the desktop
    mirrors and worklogs (#15);
  - chores at or past their frequency (the Chores screen's red), as **one line** ("6 chores due",
    their rooms by urgency) under the rest, so they never bury it (the owner, in the review: the
    tablet listed six chores and nothing else).

  Overdue items come first, then today's. A tap opens the note or the Chores tab. Mode rules
  apply: work notes hide outside Work mode.
- **Recovery verdict** in Mood & health: one line from the Life Stats readout ("In your usual
  range", "Below your usual recovery", "Better recovered than usual"). Nothing while the baseline
  is building or without data, and nothing when health is hidden (screen-share safe).
