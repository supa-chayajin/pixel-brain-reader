# 12.13.0 — The companion: the week and the month (D6, D7)

**Status:** reviewed by the owner in an interview on 2026-11-11 (gate 1: D6 runs inside the
nightly burn job and reads its own note back), then built in 12.13.0 the same day ("As built"
below). The seven decisions are recorded at the end. The checklist is
`2026-11-10-dream-app-roadmap.md` §3.

## D6 — Weekly review note

- **Where.** `10_Journal/Weekly/YYYY-Www.md`, an ISO week (Monday to Sunday, week-based year:
  the last days of December can belong to week 1 of the next year).
- **When.** In `DailyExportWorker`, right after the nightly burn, so Sunday's board is on disk
  first. A week is due once its Sunday's 23:50 run has come; a later run (a missed Sunday, the
  phone off) writes the most recent due week, never an older one. Written once: an existing note
  is never rewritten, and the week's key is remembered on the phone so a note the owner deletes
  doesn't come back. A failure is logged and never fails or retries the burn.
- **What it holds** (French, measured facts only; a section with nothing measured is left out):
  - **Habitudes:** each habit's days done out of the week.
  - **Humeur:** the week's average score, the days logged, the change from the previous week.
  - **Sommeil et pas:** average sleep over the nights recorded, average steps over the days
    recorded (the day's health JSON, like the burn's 🩺 Health section).
  - **Tâches et corvées:** tasks done out of the board's tasks (the week's burned notes), the
    distinct chores done (a chore keeps only its last-done date).
  - **XP:** gained in the week (the gamification history), the level now.
  - **Notes:** created and edited this week (git history, by author time). Personal notes by
    title, at most 10 per list; work notes only counted. Daily and weekly notes, mirrors,
    worklogs, `99_System/`, private and People notes are left out.
  - **Travail:** counts and links only: mission-log entries by type, decisions without an ADR,
    objectives that moved or stalled, meetings, the overload signal. No log text and no work
    note title. Left out while no mission log and no work folder are set.
  - **Le plan de la semaine dernière:** the previous review's "Semaine prochaine" lines, as
    written.
  - **Réflexion:** three short French prompts for the owner (what worked, what got stuck, what
    changes). The AI never writes into the note.
  - **Semaine prochaine:** an empty list, filled in on Monday.
- **Ask Coach about this week.** On an open weekly note: the week's measured facts as a French
  brief (`CoachSeeds`, ≤ 1,200 characters) handed to Chat through `ChatSeedRepository`, like the
  Objectives and Load screens. Nano can't write it in the background: AICore refuses a
  background caller (`BACKGROUND_USE_BLOCKED`, error 30, seen on the Fold on 2026-11-11).
- **Monday banner.** "Plan your week" on today's page from Monday, while the latest review's
  "Semaine prochaine" is empty and until the next review is written; it opens the note. A banner,
  not a block: the Daily order and fold don't change (gate 5).
- **Read-only device:** no note, no banner, no Coach button (#18).

## D7 — Monthly reports

- **When.** In the same job, after the burn on a month's last day; a later run writes the most
  recent due month only, once (its key remembered on the phone).
- **Which.** The work report and the personal report (`EvidenceScope`), built by
  `EvidenceRepository` like the Evidence screen's, without the AI summary (Nano refuses the
  background; the Evidence screen still writes one with its summary). Without work folders there
  is no split: one report.
- **Where.** The work report in `<first work folder>/Rapports/`, hidden outside Work mode like
  the meeting notes; the personal report in `00_Inbox/`. Without work folders, the one report
  goes to `00_Inbox/`. When the mission log is encrypted, the work report is written encrypted
  next to it (`Rapports/` beside the log, like the decision records), and needs the stored
  password like the log. Never over an existing note (`DraftWriter`, " (2)").

## The notification (opt-in)

Settings → Reminders → **Weekly review & monthly report**, off by default (gate 4): one quiet
notification when a note is written, opening it (a screen key without a parameter: the app
resolves the latest note itself). The work report's notification only in Work mode
(`muteWorkNotifications`); at 23:50 that is rare, and the report waits in the work folder.

## Tests first (gate 2)

- The round trip: a rendered note's "Semaine prochaine" reads back empty; lines the owner adds
  read back exactly and become the next review's "Le plan de la semaine dernière"; the prompts
  and placeholders never count as a plan.
- The ISO week: keys and paths around the new year (W53, W01), Monday and Sunday bounds.
- When a week or a month is due: Sunday 23:50, a missed Sunday, a run early on Sunday morning
  (not due), never an older period, never twice.
- The work section holds counts and links only: no log text, no work note title.
- The Coach brief: French, ≤ 1,200 characters, measured facts only.
- The report paths per scope and per log encryption.
- The burn's output is unchanged and a failing review leaves the burn's result as it was.

## As built (12.13.0)

- **Ask Coach about this week** sits in an open weekly review's header (`WeeklyCoachViewModel`):
  the facts are gathered again, so the brief matches the week even after the note was edited.
- **Plan your week** shows from the review's Sunday night, as soon as the note exists, until the
  plan is filled or the next review is due.
- A count of zero is left out of a line ("1 créée", not "1 créée, 0 modifiée"), and French takes
  the singular below 2.
- A month with nothing measured gets no report (`EvidenceReport.facts` empty). Each report is
  written on its own: an encrypted log without its stored password costs only the work report,
  and the month is tried again the next night.
- An encrypted log's work report gets no notification: it opens through the private vault only.
- A notification's tap carries a fixed screen key (`weekly_review`, `monthly_report`) and the app
  finds the note itself (`ReviewNoteViewModel`): no path ever travels in an intent.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-11-11, interview)

1. **When the weekly note is written:** Sunday 23:50, right after the nightly burn.
2. **The reflection:** three French prompts in the note and an "Ask Coach about this week"
   button; the AI never writes into the note.
3. **Work in the weekly note:** counts and links only.
4. **Next week:** a Monday banner until the section is filled, and the plan carried into the next
   review.
5. **Where the automatic work report goes:** the first work folder (the inbox when none is set).
6. **Which monthly reports:** the work report and the personal report.
7. **Notification:** one opt-in switch in Reminders for the weekly review and the monthly reports.

**Defaults taken (not asked):** an encrypted mission log keeps its automatic report encrypted
beside it; private and People notes are never listed; no backfill beyond the latest due period.
