# 13.1 — A game mode for every evening

**Status:** released as 13.1.0 on the morning of 2026-11-16
([v13.1.0](https://github.com/supa-chayajin/pixel-brain-reader/releases/tag/v13.1.0), APK +
mapping), built while the owner played, from two interviews in the same session. The owner asked for "a bulletproof game mode for my usage" before the signed
APK. The vault side (the game pages) lives in the private vault; nothing here names a game the
owner plays.

Unless a path starts with `app/` or `documentation/`, it is relative to
`app/src/main/java/cloud/wafflecommons/pixelbrainreader/`.

## Where it starts (13.0.0)

- **Pages:** 12.17 lists any note with `game_template` in its frontmatter, wherever it lives,
  `99_System/` included: a template note there would show as a game.
- **Templates:** six labels (D5 of 12.17); any other key shows as "Other" on the page and nothing
  on the card.
- **PSN is blocked by Sony** (`4102`, since 2026-11-15; corrected on 2026-11-27: the build's client
  secret was mistyped, Sony never blocked it, 13.2.3), so the sessions started on the phone are
  the only record of play. A session nobody stops counts on; a forgotten one can't be added, and a
  wrong one can't be fixed or removed.
- **The session picker** is a plain list in a dialog: past a dozen games, the ones below the
  dialog's height can't be reached.
- **The running session's notification** isn't put back after a reboot or a swipe.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-11-16)

1. **E1 Templates.** Two more: Platformer and Action-adventure; the games that fit none are Other
   (`other` in the vault).
2. **E2 An app update.** The app learns the new labels and skips `99_System/`, so the template
   notes can live in `99_System/Templates/`.
3. **E3 Starting states.** Backlog, except the ones being played (Playing, undated) and one
   awaited game (Next). On the phone a tap on Playing or Done dates the page; states set in the
   vault stay undated.
4. **E4 A forgotten session.** At 3 hours the phone asks "Still playing?". Keep playing asks again
   an hour later. With no answer within the hour, the session ends at the time the question was
   due, not at the time someone notices.
5. **E5 Fixing sessions.** All four: add a session that was never started, start one "10, 20 or 30
   minutes ago", change a session's start or length, delete one.
6. **E6 Play time before the app.** An optional `played_before` (hours) on each page: the cards
   show it plus the sessions, and PSN's own total replaces both once PSN measures the game.

From the second interview, the same morning:

7. **E7 The question during a game night.** It vibrates, with no sound, through Do Not Disturb:
   noticed in hand, unlikely to wake anyone.
8. **E8 Checklists on the games being played.** Sourced from the wikis and hidden until tapped,
   which needs a marker of their own while PSN (and so the trophy markers) is blocked.

## The design

### T1. Templates (E1, E2)

- `GameTemplate.PLATFORMER` (`platformer`) and `ACTION_ADVENTURE` (`action-adventure`); keys are
  persisted in the pages.
- `GamePages.parse` returns null under `99_System/`: a template carries `game_template` like a
  page, and is never a game.

### T2. The picker

- `GamePickerDialog` scrolls (a `LazyColumn`, 360 dp at most) and gets a "Find a game" field from
  eight pages on (title and aliases, `GameNames.normalize`).
- When it starts a session: Now, 10, 20 or 30 minutes ago (E5).

### T3. "Still playing?" (E4)

- Pure: `SessionCheck` (`start|nextAskAt|askedAt`, kept in `UserPreferencesRepository`
  `game_session_check`) and `GameSessionGuard.step`: Wait, Ask (an hour to answer), End (at
  `nextAskAt`, also when Doze held the question back).
- One unique WorkManager job (`game_session_check`, REPLACE, `GameSessionCheckWorker`), scheduled
  at the start, after each question, and by Keep playing. Each run puts the running notification
  back.
- The question: channel `game_check` (default importance, no sound, vibration, `setBypassDnd`,
  E7), Keep playing and Stop through `GameReceiver` (not exported). Android lets the channel pass
  Do Not Disturb only for an app with Do Not Disturb access (asked for Game night); the channel is
  created again before each post, so access granted later applies (checked on the emulator:
  `mBypassDnd` false without access, true after it). The Games tab shows the question inline while
  it waits, with Keep playing.
- A stop with an end time (`stop(now, zone, endAt)`): the record ends at `endAt`, and "today"
  stays the moment of the stop, so an evening session ended after midnight edits its evening's
  note (D11) and never re-burns it.
- `restore()` at each return to the foreground (ProcessLifecycle ON_START): the notification, and
  the next check (a session started before 13.1 has none). An unanswered question isn't posted
  again.

### T4. Sessions fixed by hand (E5)

- `ManualSessions.record` keys a typed session like a started one (`manual:<start millis>`), so
  typing one twice replaces it. Lengths from 1 minute to 24 hours.
- `GameSessionController.addPastSession`, `editSession` (manual sessions only; the day stays) and
  `deleteSession`, then the day's line: today's note burned, an earlier day within a week edited
  (D11), older days in the data file only.
- `GamesRepository.replaceSession` swaps a session in one write, disk read first.
- The game's page lists each session, newest first: a tap on a manual one opens "Fix this
  session" (start, length, Delete with a second tap); Add opens "Add a session" (day, start,
  length; nothing in the future).

### T5. Console play time (E6)

- `played_before` in the page's frontmatter, read by `GamePages.hours` (`228`, `228.5`, `228,5`,
  `228 h`, `228h30`, `228:30`) and written by `GamePages.withPlayedBefore` (one line, dated).
- `GameHours.of`: PSN's total when PSN measured the game, else `played_before` plus the sessions.
- The page's header: "Add the console's play time" opens a dialog that checks what is typed.

### T5b. Hidden checklists (E8)

- `GameChecklists`: a line ending with `<!--spoiler-->` is `spoiler`; `ChecklistItem.masked` =
  a trophy's or a spoiler's line, unticked. The game's page shows it as ??? until tapped (one at a
  time) or ticked; the tick keeps the marker, so unticking hides it again. `GamePages.prose` drops
  checklist lines, so a hidden name never reaches the page's text in the app.

### T6. The read-only device (settled decision #18)

Nothing new writes there: the controller's writes go through `VaultWriteGate`, the worker and
`restore()` do nothing, and the page hides Add, the session taps and the play time button.

## Tests

- `GamePagesTest`: the new keys, `other`, a template under `99_System/`, `played_before` read and
  written.
- `GameSessionGuardTest`: the check's round trip, the question at 3 hours, the end at the due time

## As checked (the emulator, sandbox vault, 2026-11-16)

- The Games tab lists the 41 pages copied into the sandbox and none of the nine templates.
- "Still playing?": a session backdated to 3 h 05 in the app's preferences, then the app reopened:
  the Live Update came back and the question was posted 4 s later ("Playing since 02:00. No answer
  by 06:05: the session ends at 05:00."), with Keep playing and Stop.
- The question's channel: no sound, vibration, `mBypassDnd` true once Do Not Disturb access exists.
- The owner went through the tab, the pages, the hidden checklists and the session fixes: "everything
  looks fine".

- PSN itself (still blocked by Sony; a 13.1.x when a working client or flow exists).
