# 13.4, continued: every pending item before v14

The phone's hand-off of 2026-11-16 asked to "Plan every pending implementation except WearOS". The owner was interviewed on the
morning of 2026-11-28, while the Games batch was building. The list came from the vault's "Planned, not built yet" roadmap, the
live TODO, the ideas list and the repo plans' open boxes. Excluded: what 13.4 already holds (the Games batch, Feeds, Settings)
and Wear OS (v14, the vault's RFC-010).

## The owner's answers

### Scope and order

- **P1 All sixteen items join 13.4**, which ships when the last build lands.
- **P2 The builds, in this order:** the Games batch, then Feeds + Settings, then Games v2, then the small ones, then the second
  brain, then work & modes. Build workflows run back to back. Between two builds, the owner's session reviews, commits one
  concern at a time and checks the result on the emulator.
- **P3 Unparked for after v14:** a baseline profile, the brain map, App Functions, and a second writing device. The last one
  changes settled decision #18 and gets its own interview first.
- **P4 The 9 game pages without a console title** are games not played yet: the backlog.

### Games v2

- **GV1 A Builds & guides card on a game's page.**
  - It groups the notes of the game folder's subfolders by kind: Builds, Bosses, Quests, Guides. Each note shows its one-line
    idea, and a tap opens it in the reader.
  - A pinned build shows on the running session's card and notification.
- **GV2 The owner writes them from templates.** The card offers "New build", "New boss", "New quest" and "New guide". Each one
  fills in its template (T_Build, T_Boss, T_Quest, T_Guide in `99_System/Templates`) through the app's template form, into
  that subfolder. Ask Claude stays as it is.
- **GV3 Gaming in the stats:**
  - A Gaming card in Life Stats: hours this week and this month, a 12-week trend, the top games, and the trophies and
    achievements earned.
  - In Honest insights, sleep and mood on gaming days, with the same rules as the other insights (nothing claimed below 14
    paired days).
  - A 🎮 section in the weekly note and in the personal monthly report.
- **GV4 A Now playing widget**, from 2×2 to 4×2. While a session runs: its cover, its time, Stop, and Keep playing when asked.
  Otherwise the last game played, with its hours and Start.
- **GV5 The Game tile still starts a session at once.** For the session's first 15 minutes, its notification offers "Started
  earlier": 10, 20 or 30 minutes ago.

### The small ones

- **SM1 Health history refused** (seen in the Fold's log on 27 November): investigate it. The Health Connect history
  permission (`READ_HEALTH_DATA_HISTORY`) is approved in advance, in case the 30-day read limit is the cause; it is requested
  where the other health permissions are.
- **SM2 The file list's swipe** moves to the newer API, which removes the compiler's last warning. It is checked on the Fold.
- **SM3 CI:** setup-java v5, approved, in its own commit.
- **SM4 L4:** an image dropped or pasted into a note is resized and compressed before it lands in the vault.
- **SM5 E2:** the Mode tile shows the icon chosen for each mode.

### The second brain

- **SB1 K1, step 1:** a variable-length export of the same model. Today's vectors are kept (no re-index), and each passage
  takes about 2.5× less work.
- **SB2 K1, step 2:** the int8 model. The APK drops from about 600 MB to about 170 MB, and the notes are indexed again once.
- **SB3 The gate:** both steps are measured on the Fold. Meaning and hybrid may each lose at most 0.02, and the negatives stay
  clean. On 15 November: meaning 0.77, words 0.82, hybrid 0.93.
- **SB4 L3, Days like this.** "Days like this" on a day's page lists the 5 most similar past days (date, mood, one line), and
  a tap opens that day. Chat also answers "days like today".
  - A day document holds the day's note, its mood and tags, sleep and steps, and the games played.
  - Private notes count only while their AI switch is on.

### Work & modes

- **WM1 W3, the security watch:**
  - A watchlist in `99_System/`.
  - Once a week, one osv.dev query per package, shown in the privacy ledger.
  - The note is written every Monday in the first work folder, as `Security watch/<yyyy>-W<ww>.md`.
  - Its notification and its line in the evidence report show in Work mode only.
- **WM2 E1, per-mode widgets.** A widget hidden in the current mode shows a calm placeholder: the mode's emblem, no data. A tap
  opens the app.
- **WM3 Notifications per mode.** In Reminders, each notification gets "Shows in" buttons, like the feeds and the tabs: the
  evening check-in, the private vault reminder, chores & habits, the reviews, focus done, "Still playing?". Focus is hidden in
  Off. The work-only ones keep their rule.
- **WM4 Changed files:** Settings › Vault & device › Changed files, read-only. Each file shows its diff and when it was saved,
  with Push now.

## Without a choice

- **Every build follows 13.4's rules:**
  - Agents never commit.
  - Unit tests pass, and lint stays at 0 errors and 0 warnings.
  - No names: this repository is public.
  - The read-only device writes nothing.
  - Every new network caller goes through the privacy ledger. osv.dev gets a new persisted key, `security_watch`.
- **K1 needs the Fold:** the owner runs Measure retrieval on it, and step 2 ships only after that.
- **The four templates** are written in the vault with French headings, like the other templates.
- **The weekly and monthly 🎮 sections** use French headings, like the rest of those notes, with links to the game pages.
- **The watchlist** has one package per line, `ecosystem: name`, with an optional version.
