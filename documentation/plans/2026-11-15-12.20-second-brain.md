## Design

### R2 — Hybrid search

- **`data/ai/HybridRanking` (pure):** reciprocal-rank fusion, where rank r (from 0) weighs
  1 / (60 + r + 1). It fuses the meaning search's top 20 passages, all above the floor, with the
  ranks of their notes in the full-text search's top 20 (FTS4 any-term, `fullTextHits(…, anyTerm =
  true)`).
  - Every result is one of the meaning search's passages (D2).
  - Every passage of a note gets its note's word rank.
  - Equal scores keep the meaning order.
- **`data/ai/NoteRetrieval`:** what Chat calls.
  - Off, it is the vector search alone at the size asked, as before.
  - On, it takes 20 candidates by meaning; nothing above the floor stays empty without asking the
    words (#2). It fuses and then takes the size asked.
  - A failing word search leaves the meaning order.
- **The switch:** `UserPreferencesRepository.hybridSearch` (`hybrid_search`, on by default,
  device-only). Settings → Knowledge Vault, under "Private notes in AI answers".
- **The harness (`GoldenSetRunner`):**
  - One search of each kind at 20 candidates. The meaning and words columns keep their first 8, as
    before, so the numbers above stay comparable.
  - `hybrid` is the production fusion over all the candidates. The line becomes
    "…meaning x, words y, hybrid h, either z…".
  - Negatives are the same for the hybrid by construction.
- **The ship rule** (plan §4: "R2 and K1 ship only if the golden set says so"): on the Fold before
  the tag, the hybrid must be at least the meaning's recall, with negatives no worse.

### The Chat revamp (C1–C4)

`ui/ai/ChatPanel.kt`, in Material 3 Expressive:

- **Persona colors:** a `ChatPalette` per mode gives the container and on-container pairs from the
  dynamic scheme, so text keeps the scheme's contrast.
- **Bubbles:** the question in the persona's container color, right-aligned. The answer on the
  persona's tinted surface, left-aligned under a small avatar and the persona's name, with the
  Markdown inside.
  - Corners are large, with a small corner on the sender's side.
  - Consecutive bubbles of one side tighten the corners between them.
- **Inside an answer:** the sources as chips of note titles with their folder (`projectDisplayName`
  rules for numbered folders), then Copy and Save to Inbox.
- **Thinking:** the Expressive `LoadingIndicator` in an answer bubble until the first words;
  `loadingStage` stays as its label.
- **Motion:** new bubbles spring in (`MaterialTheme.motionScheme`); `animateItem` stays.
- **Empty screen:** the persona's large avatar in its container color, its name, what it does.
- **Kept:** the segmented persona switch, the input (text state stays synchronous), dictation, the
  Nano line, Clear, the preset menu.

### K2 — Ask this note / this folder

- **The scope:** `ChatScope` is a note path or a folder prefix, held by `ChatViewModel` with a
  clearable chip above the input. A seed hands it over, like Ask Coach.
- **A note:** its text, read from disk, when it fits the prompt budget. Otherwise chained summaries
  (D6): parts cut between paragraphs, one summary per part for the question, then the answer over
  the summaries, with progress shown in the thinking label.
- **A folder:** retrieval restricted to the folder's passages (meaning, then words as in R2).
- **Prompts:** in French, ending with `AiLanguage.DIRECTIVE`.
- **History:** answers in Chat's history with their scope (D7). No Ask on a private note, a
  `99_Private/` folder, or on a read-only device (no AI there).
- **Continue:** a chip when an answer stops at the output cap (the stream's finish reason).
- **Entry points (D5):** the note header's Ask button and the folder menu's "Ask this folder".

### K3 — Time Machine v2

- **Renames:** history follows them (`FollowFilter` with rename detection).
- **Word-level highlights:** inside the changed lines, a pure word diff over the line diff.
- **"Since your last sync" (D4):** each pull records the HEAD before and after it, on the device.
  - A note's history opens on what the last pull changed in it.
  - The file list marks the notes that pull changed.
  - A pull that brought nothing leaves the previous mark.

### K5 — Related while writing

- **What it shows:** the 3 notes closest to the paragraph around the cursor, by meaning; never the
  note itself.
- **When it runs:** 1.5 s after typing stops, off the main thread behind the model's fair lock, and
  cancelled by the next keystroke.
- **Where:** a side pane on a wide window, chips above the keyboard otherwise. A tap inserts
  `[[link]]` at the cursor; a long-press opens the note (D8).
- **Opt-out:** a "Hide related" choice in the editor's menu, kept on the device.

## Not in 12.20

- W4's E3, E6, E7 and E1 go to 12.21 the same evening; E2, the tile's custom mode icon, isn't
  planned.
- L4's compression of dropped images.
- **Two costs the emulator showed, older than 12.20:**
  - A young note's Time Machine reads the vault's whole history: JGit rewrites the oldest
    commit's parents down to the root. This took about 2.5 minutes on the emulator.
    - Stopping at the note's creation would hide a deleted note's earlier life, which the
      Time Machine shows on purpose (`JGitProviderHistoryTest`): that is a decision for the owner.
  - Editing a note marks its passages stale, and that changes the vector index's signature, so
    the next search re-reads every vector once (2 minutes on the emulator for K5's first related
    search).
    - Since 12.20, a reload a cancelled search started runs to its end. Next step: a signature
      that a stale mark doesn't change.
