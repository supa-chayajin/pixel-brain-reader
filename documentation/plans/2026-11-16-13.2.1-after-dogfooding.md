## Decisions (owner, 2026-11-16)

1. **D1 PSN.** Stop watching. PSN stays as it is (sessions by hand work), and the session-start check
   of the community libraries is dropped. The last check, that night: the report of Sony refusing the
   public client (30 September) had no news, psn-api was still on 2.18.1 and psnawp on 3.1.0.
2. **N1 The open tab's emblem.** Its shape and colour came from a hash of the tab's name, so any
   order of the tabs gave a random run of shapes and colours. The owner first picked shapes and
   colours by place, then asked before the build: "could it be set randomly in order to always change
   shape even for the same tab? ONLY the daily must stay as is". As decided:
   - a shape drawn at random each time a tab opens, never the one shown just before (even back on the
     same tab), from the rounded expressive shapes that leave the icon room;
   - the colour by place (primary, secondary, tertiary in turn);
   - the tab page's header shows the same emblem;
   - Daily keeps its turning sun.
3. **H1 Maximizing a hub's page.** "I should be able to hide the list to have only the selected
   project (as we did for an open note)", then "don't do the same thing as for the note, it was just to
   explain to maximize it when I want". Material 3's drag handle on the divider: a tap hides the list
   or brings it back, and a drag stops at the list's width, half and half, or the whole page. All three
   hubs (Projects, Boards, Runbooks), which share the layout.
4. **H2 No back arrow beside the list.** "When in large screen mode, the project page should not show
   the back arrow like on the phone as it is useless on large screen." Side by side, a hub's page gets
   no close action and draws no arrow; Back and the list still close it. The three hubs' pages share
   the layout, so all three follow; a phone or a portrait tablet, where the page covers the list, keeps
   the arrow.
5. **When.** Built that evening as 13.2.1, released later with the other fixes from the owner's
   dogfooding.

## As built

- **N1:** `ui/components/TabEmblems.kt` holds `OpenTabEmblem` (the tab's name, its place, the drawn
  shape), `LocalOpenTabEmblem`, `TabEmblems.shapes` and `TabShapeDraws`. The 14 shapes are the five
  cookies, two clovers, sunny, soft burst, flower, puffy, puffy diamond, gem and pentagon.
  `TabEmblems.nextShape` never returns the last shape, and `placeTone` gives a place's tone.
  - `MainScreen` draws a shape each time the open tab changes (`openTabOf(navDestinations(…))`, a
    `remember` keyed on the tab's name, so a page under its tab keeps the draw) and provides it.
  - `SlidingEmblem` pops into the drawn shape and the place's tone as it arrives. `NavPiece` tints
    the open icon with the place's tone.
  - `CortexTopAppBar`'s heading takes the drawn emblem when its emblem key is the open tab's name.
    `ExpressiveBadge` takes a polygon.
- **H1:** `HubListDetail` gets `rememberPaneExpansionState` (keyed on the scaffold value) and
  `HubSplit.anchors`: hidden, the list's width, and half and half when it sits at least 96 dp from the
  list's width (it doesn't on the unfolded Fold). `HubSplitHandle` is `VerticalDragHandle` with
  `paneExpansionDraggable` and a tap that toggles between hidden and the list's width. It shows only
  while a page is open side by side, and closing the page clears the split. No new dependency:
  adaptive-layout 1.2.0 and material3 1.5.0-alpha23 were already resolved.
  - Coming back to a hub finds it as it was left: tab navigation saves the hub's state, the open page
    and the split together. The interview had said the split would reset on leaving; a reset when the
    hub leaves the screen doesn't reach the saved state, and keeping the page and its split together
    reads better. The owner can still ask for a reset.
- **H2:** `HubListDetail`'s `detail` gets a null close action side by side. `ProjectScreen`,
  `BoardScreen` and `RunbookPlayerScreen` take a nullable `onBack` and draw no arrow without one.
- **The compiler:** a redundant annotation target in `PeopleRepository` (12.19) is gone, so the only
  warning left is `FileListPane`'s deliberate swipe one.
- **Tests:** `TabEmblemsTest`, `OpenTabTest` and `HubSplitTest`, 14 in all.
- **Checked on the tablet** (the real vault, read-only, a debug build), in landscape:
  - the rail's emblem and the page header match, and change at each visit: Projects a soft burst,
    then a puffy clover after a visit to Stats; Stats a tertiary pentagon, its header too;
  - a project opened beside the list has no back arrow;
  - the handle hides the list (the page takes the width), a second tap brings it back, and a drag
    snaps to half and half;
  - leaving for Habits and coming back keeps the project maximized.
  
  In portrait (800 dp): one pane, the bar's emblem, and the page's back arrow.
