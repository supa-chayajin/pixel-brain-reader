# 12.19 — People & 1:1s (plan, 2026-11-15)

The roadmap's 12.19 item (`2026-11-10-dream-app-roadmap.md`): **P1 People & 1:1s** (v11 C10).
Each person gets an encrypted note, a meeting's prep shows the person it's with, and a reminder
comes before a 1:1. The owner reviewed it in an interview on 2026-11-15, right after the 12.18.0
release.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-11-15)

- **D1 Who.** Work and personal people. Each note is marked work or personal. Work people show
  in Work mode only; personal people show in every mode.
- **D2 Where.** A People tab in the private journal, with the journal's protections (no
  screenshots, the auto-lock, Back locks). The toolkit's People tool opens it.
- **D3 File names.** Neutral ids (`99_Private/People/person-<id>.md.enc`): nothing in git says
  who a note is about. Names appear only after an unlock.
- **D4 A fresh start.** No existing notes to import.
- **D5 Matching by hand.** In a meeting's prep, "This is with…" links the recurring event to a
  person. The phone remembers the link.
- **D6 The 1:1 reminder.** The 10-minute meeting reminder stays. A new reminder comes 30 minutes
  before events linked to a person and events titled like a 1:1. Work people's events and 1:1
  titles notify in Work mode only; personal people's events in any mode. A switch in Settings →
  Reminders, on by default.
- **D7 The write-up.** A dated entry in the person's 1:1 log. For a linked event, the prep's
  button becomes "Write the 1:1 note".
- **D8 What a note holds.** Role, context, open points, agreed actions (yours and theirs), the
  1:1 log, feedback (given and received, dated), and personal details.
- **D9 Extras.**
  - **Log a decision:** a work person's decision goes to the mission log, decided by their role,
    never by their name.
  - **My action to tasks:** one of your actions becomes a task for today, in words you edit first.
  - **Ask Coach to prepare:** the role, the open points and the actions go to Gemini Nano on the
    phone; the name is left out.
- **D10 Timing.** Built the same morning; the owner checks it on the Fold.

## What the design rests on (checked 2026-11-15)

- **The folder.** `VaultSchema.PEOPLE_FOLDER` (`99_Private/People/`) is already never embedded
  (`IndexingWorker`), and never in the weekly review or a focus session's notes
  (`WeeklyActivity.excluded`). Settled decision #20 keeps People notes out of the AI either way;
  an explicit brief the owner sends stays allowed (D9's Coach).
- **The format.** `CryptoManager` is unchanged: salt, IV, AES-GCM, PBKDF2 at 210k rounds. Each
  file keeps the salt of the process that wrote it. Reading N people costs at most N key
  derivations, run in parallel and cached until the journal locks. A change of password (12.18
  V1) already re-encrypts every `*.md.enc`, People included.
- **The password check.** An unlock decrypts one of the two newest notes in `99_Private/` itself,
  and an empty journal accepts any password. A People note in its subfolder escaped that check:
  with an empty journal, a typo could encrypt new People notes under the wrong password. The check
  now samples the newest `.md.enc` anywhere under `99_Private/`.
- **The confidentiality guard** checks a new file's path against the deny-list, and can't read
  encrypted content. Neutral ids never trip it.
- **The calendar.**
  - The import fetches today's events with `singleEvents`. A recurring event's instance is named
    `<series id>_<start>`; a single event's id has no `_`.
  - `MeetingSeries.keyOf(eventId)` takes the part before `_`, so a link follows the whole series.
    It needs no database column and doesn't touch the daily note's format (settled decision #7).
  - The import keeps no invitees, so the matching is by hand (D5).
- **The background can't read People notes** (settled decision #21). A reminder knows only the
  event's calendar title and the phone's links (a series key → a person id and work/personal, no
  names). A notification never shows an open point: it appears on the lock screen.
- **Meeting prep runs in `MainActivity`**, a `FragmentActivity`. The prep can open the session with
  the journal's prompt (BiometricPrompt + CryptoObject, face first when strong, then fingerprint,
  then the password).
- **Chat keeps its history in Room**, in clear. A 1:1 brief must not land there, so Coach answers
  in a sheet under `FLAG_SECURE`, and nothing is saved.

## Design

### The note (`data/people/PersonNote`, pure)

- **Frontmatter.** The vault's DTO (the name as `title`), plus `kind: pro|perso` and `role:`.
- **Sections, in French** like the vault's other app-written notes:
  - `## Contexte`, `## Personnel`: free text;
  - `## Points ouverts`, `## Mes actions`, `## Ses actions`: checklists (`- [ ] text (date)`,
    done `- [x] text (date) ✅ date`);
  - `## Feedback`: `- date — donné : …` or `- date — reçu : …`;
  - `## 1:1`: one `### yyyy-MM-dd` entry per meeting, newest first.
- **Round trip.** Unknown sections and lines are kept as they are.

### The repository (`data/people/PeopleRepository`)

- **Reading.** It lists `person-*.md.enc`, decrypting each with a copy of the session's password,
  in parallel. A file that doesn't open is listed as unreadable, never dropped. The list stays in
  memory and is dropped when the session locks.
- **Writing.** Each change reads the file from disk, applies the change, then encrypts and writes
  it atomically through `PrivateNoteRepository` (the gated writer, under the session's write lock).
  Then it requests a push. On a read-only device every write is refused.

### Unlocking outside the journal (`data/privatevault/PrivateVaultUnlocker`)

The journal's unlock sequence, shared with meeting prep: verify the password against the notes,
open the session, bind or confirm (12.18 V3), then the follow-ups.

### The People tab (`ui/privatevault/`)

- **Tabs.** Notes | People under the journal's bar; the toolkit's People tool opens the journal on
  People.
- **The list.** Each person shows their name, Work or Personal, their role, and "3 open points ·
  2 actions · last 1:1 12 Nov". Outside Work mode the work people are hidden, and one line says
  so. The + button adds a person (name, work or personal, role).
- **A person's page.**
  - Context;
  - open points (add, tick, delete);
  - your actions (tick, "Add to today's tasks") and theirs;
  - the 1:1 log (a new entry dated today, edit an entry);
  - feedback given and received;
  - personal details.
  - Its menu: edit, delete, Log a decision (work people), Ask Coach to prepare.
- **On a read-only device** the pages are view-only.

### Meeting prep (`ui/meetings/`)

- **The person card.**
  - Locked: "Unlock to see who it's with", with the face prompt; "Use password" asks in a dialog.
  - Unlocked and linked: the person's role, open points, both sides' actions and the last 1:1's
    date.
  - Unlocked and not linked: "This is with…" lists the people the mode shows, or adds a new
    person.
  - Unlink is in the card.
- **The link** lives on the phone (`UserPreferencesRepository.personLinks`: series key → person
  id and kind).
- **"Write the 1:1 note"** opens the journal on that person with a new 1:1 entry dated the
  meeting's day.
- **While the card shows,** the prep holds the session (like the journal, so it doesn't lock
  mid-meeting) and sets `FLAG_SECURE`.

### The 1:1 reminder (`data/meetings/OneOnOneReminders`)

- **Which events.** Pure: today's entries linked to a person, or titled like a 1:1 ("1:1", "1-1",
  "1on1", "one-on-one", "one to one", "tête-à-tête", "point individuel").
- **When.** 30 minutes before, one unique job each (`one_on_one_<eventId>`, tag `one_on_one`).
  Rescheduled after each calendar import, when the switch changes, and when a link changes.
- **Posting.** The worker posts only if the event is still there. Work people's events and 1:1
  titles post in Work mode only (`muteWorkNotifications`); personal people's events post in any
  mode.
- **The notification.** "<title> in 30 min", on its own channel ("1:1 reminders"). A tap opens the
  prep. It never shows a name from a note or an open point.

### Extras (D9)

- **Log a decision** (work people): the decision and "Decided by" (their role, editable) become a
  `décision` in the mission log (`MissionLogRepository.add`). An encrypted log works because the
  session is open.
- **My action to tasks:** the text in an editable dialog that says the task will be plain text,
  then `TaskRepository.addTask(…, today)`.
- **Ask Coach to prepare:** `OneOnOneCoach.brief`, pure and French, at most 1,200 characters:
  - in: the role, work or personal, the open points, both sides' open actions and the last 1:1's
    date;
  - out: the name, the context, the personal details and the feedback;
  - then `AiLanguage.DIRECTIVE`.
  - It runs through `LocalAiManager.generateResponse`, and the answer shows in a sheet under
    `FLAG_SECURE`, never saved.
  - It's off on a read-only device, which has no AI.

## Mood tags by category (owner, 2026-11-15, the same morning)

The owner asked for it before 12.19 ships, in an interview of its own. It's built first.

### Decisions

- **M1 Three categories.** Activities, People and Places, fixed. They're separate from the icon
  picker's six groups.
- **M2 The owner's tags**, sorted once in the vault's `mood_tags.json`:
  - Activities: Gaming, Coding, Working, Chilling, Playtime, Chores, Self-Care, Event,
    Brainstorming;
  - People: Solo, Kid, Family, Date;
  - Places: Home, CDS, Out.
- **M3 New tags** get a category when they're added, preselected from the icon and changeable any
  time on the Mood Tags page:
  - an icon from the picker's People group → People;
  - one from Places & travel → Places;
  - any other → Activities.
- **M4 The check-in sheet** shows a card per category, each with its own color and an icon header.
- **M5 The rest of the sheet:**
  - the picked face's pill and the Save button take the mood's color (warm when low, green when
    high);
  - bigger faces that bounce when picked;
  - a more colorful sheet overall.
- **M6 It ships inside 12.19.**

### Design

- **The file.**
  - `categories` (tag → `activities`, `people` or `places`) sits next to `tags` and `icons`, as
    the icons did in 12.7.0, so an older version still reads the list.
  - A tag with no entry shows the icon's guess (M3) until one is chosen.
  - A key this version doesn't know is kept as written, and shows the guess meanwhile.
  - Nothing is written until an edit.
  - 12.18.0 rewrites the file without `categories` at its next tag edit. After the one-time sort
    (M2), tags are best edited with 12.19 installed; otherwise only the guess's two misses
    (Chilling, CDS) need moving back.
- **The Mood Tags page.**
  - The tags are grouped under the three categories, each with an icon header and a count.
  - Each row: the icon, the name, its category (a menu), up and down within its category, remove.
  - The add row gains a category choice, preselected from the icon until one is picked.
- **The sheet** (`MoodCheckInSheet`):
  - larger faces (36 sp) with an expressive spring when one is picked, and the pill behind the
    picked face in the mood's color;
  - three cards in the theme's own tones, so light and dark both work: Activities in tertiary,
    People in secondary, Places in primary; a picked chip takes its card's strong color;
  - the Save button in the mood's color;
  - `MoodPalette` (`ui/mood`): one container and on-container pair per score, for light and dark.
- **Past entries** keep their tags by name. History and Daily don't change.

### Checks

- **JVM:**
  - the file's round trip with categories: an unknown key kept, no `categories` written when none
    was chosen;
  - the guess from the icon;
  - moving a tag within its category.
- **Emulator:** the sheet in light and dark, the Mood Tags page, a tag added with a category.
- **Owner:** the sheet on the Fold.
