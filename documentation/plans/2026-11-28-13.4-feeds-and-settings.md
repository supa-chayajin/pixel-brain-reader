# 13.4, continued: the Feeds tab, and Settings rebuilt

Asked from the phone in two hand-offs on 2026-11-17: "Having a dedicated page for the RSS News and
being able to select feeds depending on the selected mode" and "Revamp and re-organize Settings
pages (ALL OF THEM)". The owner was interviewed on the morning of 2026-11-28, while the Games batch
(`2026-11-27-13.4-games-pictures-and-audit.md`) was building. The owner said: "As long as I say
today, we're adding features". So both join 13.4.0, released late tonight with the Games batch.

The questions were grounded in two read-only surveys: a map of today's Settings (7 sections, 14
sub-pages, about 190 controls, about 15 settings that live inside features) and the tech watch code
(`data/watch/`, `ui/settings/FeedsScreen.kt`, `ui/daily/WatchSection.kt`). During the interview the
owner also reported that each Settings category cuts the text of its first and last entries.

## The owner's answers: Feeds

- **F1 A tab of its own**, named **Feeds**. It goes at the end of the bar order, as Projects and
  Games did, and each mode can hide it like any other tab.
- **F2 One stream, newest first.** The current mode's feeds are mixed together, and each headline
  shows its feed and age. Chips on top filter by feed and show its unread count. A tap opens the
  reader, and the inbox button imports.
- **F3 Unread marks, per device.** An opened headline is dimmed, and "Mark all read" clears the
  rest. Nothing goes into the vault, so the read-only tablet keeps its own marks.
- **F4 Each feed picks its modes.** The feeds page gives each feed "Shows in" buttons named after
  the owner's three modes. Today's Work switch becomes "Work only", and a new feed shows in all
  three.
- **F5 Daily's block** shows the 5 newest unread headlines across the mode's feeds, then "See all"
  to the tab. It stays where the owner placed it.
- **F6 History:** every item a feed carries, dropping anything older than 30 days. Today the app
  keeps only the 20 newest per feed.
- **F7 Pictures:** a thumbnail beside each headline when the feed gives one. It is loaded from the
  article's site through the public-address guard and counted in the privacy ledger.

## The owner's answers: Settings

- **S1 Sections by topic,** all on one page and nothing folded: Your day, Modes, Life OS, Toolkit
  (under the owner's name for it), Connections, AI & search, Privacy & safety, Vault & device. The
  owner approved the full map below as shown.
- **S2 Modes becomes a hub of short pages.** Each row shows its current value and opens its page.
- **S3 One Navigation page:** each tab's place in the order (by dragging) and the modes it shows
  in, plus the rail's names. Modes' "Tabs per mode" moves here.
- **S4 Settings that live inside features.** Small device switches also get a control in Settings:
  the journal's auto-lock, related notes while writing, the rail's names. Feature settings get a
  row that opens the feature: the mission log, meetings, boards, projects, pinned tools.
- **S5 Reordering by drag:** long-press a ≡ handle, then drag. Screen readers get "Move up" and
  "Move down" actions instead.
- **S6 A search field** on the home page. It finds any setting by its name or description and opens
  its page at that setting.
- **S7 Import / Export All become repair tools** in Vault & device: "Reload habits, rooms & chores
  from the vault" and "Write them back to the vault". Each asks for confirmation, with a line
  saying that changes already save on their own.
- **S8 A Games page** in Connections, with four rows:
  - PlayStation and Xbox, each with its status, opening its page.
  - Game night: what it does, and Do Not Disturb access. It is listed even in a build with no
    console.
  - "Unlock to start a session", on by default (G2 of the Games batch).
- **S9 The truncation (the owner's report, confirmed).** Each category is clipped to 32 dp rounded
  corners. On entries without an inner margin (intro texts, the version line, Self-test, Measure
  retrieval, the Import / Export buttons), the curve eats the start of the first and last lines.
  Every entry now gets its own segment shape and an inner margin wider than the curve. Loose texts
  and buttons move into proper rows.
- **S10 A Moods page** holds the face set (with its preview) and the tags (icons and categories).
  RPG rules keeps the health goals and the tag-to-stat rules.
- **S11 A Permissions page** in Privacy & safety. For each permission the app uses, it shows what
  the permission is for and whether it is granted, with a button that asks for it or opens the
  system's page.

## Without a choice

Feeds:

- **Keys.**
  - The tab's key is `feeds`. It is persisted, so never rename it.
  - Each feed in `feeds.json` gains `modes`, holding the mode keys `work`, `personal` and `off`.
    When `modes` is absent, `work` decides: `true` means Work only, otherwise all three modes.
  - `work` is still written (true only for a Work-only feed), so an older app keeps its rule.
  - A feed shows in at least one mode.
- **Screen-share safe** hides the feeds that show only in Work, as the Work switch did.
- **Unread marks** are kept in DataStore and pruned along with the cache. Opening a headline (in the
  reader or the browser) or importing it marks it read. The tab gets a dot when headlines have
  arrived since it was last opened.
- **Refresh rules are unchanged:** when the app comes to the foreground, when the mode changes, and
  when a sync ends; never a worker. Pull to refresh does what "Check now" does.
- **Thumbnails:**
  - Taken from the item's image (media, enclosure or itunes image) and stored in the cache's
    existing `thumbnailUrl` column, so Room is unchanged.
  - https only, public addresses only, a size cap, redirects checked at every hop.
  - Counted under a new persisted ledger key, `feed_pictures` ("Feed pictures").
  - Shown on the tab only: Daily's block stays text.
- **Daily's block** keeps its key `watch`, and its title becomes Feeds, like the tab.
- **On a read-only device:** the tab works, since the marks are the device's own. There is no
  Import (a tap opens the browser), and the feeds page shows ReadOnlyNotice.

Settings:

- **One frame and one row for all of Settings.**
  - Every page uses FormScaffold + CortexTopAppBar, with the page's icon and its section as the
    subtitle.
  - A row's title and icon are those of the page it opens. Copy is in sentence case.
  - Each row's description names what its page holds.
  - Mode names are never hard-coded. Android's own Modes are called "Android's Modes".
- **Lists and controls.**
  - Every list is segmented.
  - A 2- to 4-way choice is a connected button group: a habit's days, a tag's category, a
    conflict's choice.
  - One time picker everywhere.
  - Back arrows, never a close cross, and room left for the floating bar.
- **On a read-only device:** ReadOnlyNotice sits at the top of every page whose action writes the
  vault. Health Connect, Google and Reminders say what doesn't run there.
- **The habit editor:** "New habit" when the habit is new. "Archive" (what Delete does today) only
  on an existing habit. Automatic sources shown by name.
- **RPG rules:** a line saying what the goals and the rules do, each stat by its name, and sliders
  on round steps.
- **Leftovers removed:** the unused ViewModel state and preference keys the survey found.
- **Settled decision #17 still holds:** a wide window shows one centred column, in this order. The
  Settings paragraph of CLAUDE.md changes, with the owner's OK of 2026-11-28.

## The design's questions (the owner, 2026-11-28)

The design left ten questions. Nine kept its default:

- On the first start of 13.4, the cached headlines and each feed's first full read start unread.
  One "Mark all read" clears them.
- A feed keeps at most 500 headlines within its 30 days, the newest ones.
- A picture given over http is never upgraded to https: it shows no thumbnail.
- When everything is read, Daily's Feeds block stays, with "Nothing new since you last looked." and
  See all. It goes only when the mode has no feed or nothing is cached.
- "Mark all read" marks the current view: the selected feed's chip, or every feed of the mode
  under All.
- The tab groups the headlines under day headers (Today, Yesterday, then the date), with an All
  chip before the feeds' chips.
- The one-option "Local AI" radio goes, with its `ai_model_selection` key. The Gemini Nano page
  names the model in one line.
- On the Navigation page, a tab may be hidden in all three modes.
- The Private journal page's Change password row opens the journal, where Change password stays in
  the menu.

One answer changed the default: **the theme always follows the system** (settled decision #12).
The stored `app_theme_config` override goes, and a value an old version saved is cleared once.
