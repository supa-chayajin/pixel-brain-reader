# 12.12.0 — The companion: meetings and the watch (D4, D5)

**Status:** reviewed by the owner on 2026-11-11 (gate 1: D4 reads retrieval; D5 fetches from the
network). The four decisions are recorded at the end. The checklist is
`2026-11-10-dream-app-roadmap.md` §3.

## D4 — Meeting prep

- **Entry point.** A calendar entry on today's timeline (one the Google import brought, so it
  carries a `googleEventId`) opens its prep. A local entry keeps opening the edit dialog. Route
  `meeting_prep?entry=<id>`.
- **The prep page**, from what the vault already holds:
  - **Notes about it.** The title without its `[Calendar]` tag is searched two ways and fused the
    way "Explain this error" does it (`ErrorLookup.merge`): full-text with the words OR-ed and
    ranked (`FtsQuery.buildAny`), then the vector hits above the 0.35 floor (#2). At most 6, with
    an excerpt each. Hybrid retrieval (R2) replaces the fusion when it ships.
  - **The last decisions about it.** Mission-log entries typed `décision` and the ADRs in
    `Décisions/` whose text matches the title, newest first, 3 at most.
  - **Write the minutes.** A new note from the vault's meeting template when there is one (plain
    otherwise), in the project the top note belongs to (`ProjectFormat.projectFor`), else
    `00_Inbox/`, through `DraftWriter` (never overwrites). The timeline entry gets one line
    linking to it, like a voice capture.
- **Modes.** Work notes and the mission log stay hidden outside Work mode (`Visibility`), like
  every work surface: the page then shows only what the mode allows, and says so.
- **The notification** (opt-in, off by default, Work mode only through `muteWorkNotifications`):
  "Standup in 10 min · 3 notes, 1 decision", opening the prep. Its timing is decision 1, its
  silence decision 2 (both below).
- **Read-only device:** the page works (it only reads); no notification and no minutes button.
- **Not in D4:** people's open points (they come with P1).
- **Tests:** the title clean-up (tag, dates, recurrence words), the fusion order, the decision
  filter, the mode rules, the route.

## D5 — Tech watch (RFC-003 revived)

- **Feeds** move out of the code: `NewsRepository` hard-codes a personal list in this public
  repository (gate 7 says the feed list lives in the private vault). They go to
  `10_Journal/data/config/feeds.json` (`{"feeds": [{"url", "name", "work"}]}`, like
  `projects.json`: every change reads the disk first, an unreadable file is moved aside), edited
  in Settings by a new row in an existing section (sections never move). How the file starts is
  decision 3.
- **The block** `watch` (a persisted `DailySection` key, never renamed) arrives in "More today".
  Headlines grouped by feed, 3 per feed, each with **Read** (the share screen's reader for that
  URL: reader mode, then Import from there) and **Import** (straight to the inbox, the share
  reader's clip). A feed marked `work` hides outside Work mode (decision 4).
- **Freshness:** refreshed when the app comes to the foreground, through the Room cache (`NewsDao`),
  at most every 2 hours; never on a timer, never a worker.
- **Safety:** a feed URL is fetched like a shared page: `ImportUrlValidator` and public addresses
  only, every redirect checked, through the guarded loader, and the XML is parsed from the text
  (RssParser's own client would skip the guard). A feed that fails shows its last headlines, dated.
- **Clean-up:** `DailyBriefingRepository` and the old `NewsRepository` are used nowhere; they go.
- **Read-only device:** the block shows (it reads the network, never the vault); the feeds are
  edited on the phone.
- **Tests:** the config's read/move-aside/round trip, the per-feed cap and grouping, the mode
  filter, the URL guard on a feed, the 2-hour cache rule.

## As built (12.12.0)

- **Write the meeting note** uses the toolkit's own meeting note (`MeetingRepository.create`: its
  folder setting, `<first work folder>/Réunions` by default, and its format) instead of a new note
  from the vault template in a project folder: a second meeting-note format would have stayed out
  of Meetings & requests, Next actions and the weekly load. The prep goes before its agenda.
- The notification's tap is `pixelbrain://open?screen=meeting_prep&entry=<uuid>`, the only screen
  key with a parameter, which must be a canonical UUID.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-11-11)

1. **D4 notification timing: WorkManager**, like every reminder: no new permission; Doze may
   deliver it a few minutes late, inside the 10-minute lead.
2. **D4 quiet hours: Android's Do Not Disturb.** No new setting; Work mode only already keeps it
   out of the evenings.
3. **D5 first feed list: seeded from today's list.** The six feeds are written once into the
   vault's `feeds.json` (a local vault commit the owner pushes) and leave the public code.
4. **D5 work feeds: a per-feed Work flag.** A feed marked Work hides outside Work mode; the others
   show in every mode.
