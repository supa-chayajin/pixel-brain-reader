# 12.14.0 — The companion: the end of work (D8, D9)

**Status:** reviewed by the owner in an interview on 2026-11-11 (D8 burns the day's note and
moves tasks; D9 adds a permission, #9). The eight decisions are recorded at the end. The checklist
is `2026-11-10-dream-app-roadmap.md` §3.

## D8 — Shutdown ritual

- **When.** On work days (Modes' work days), from 30 minutes before work ends (Modes' work end:
  17:30 for 18:00) until the owner closes the day or midnight, on today's page only. Its work parts
  (the win) hide with Work mode at the end of work; the rest stays. It reads Modes' work days and
  hours whether or not the schedule switches modes. A banner, not a block: the Daily order and
  fold don't change (gate 5). Never on a read-only device.
- **Unfinished tasks.** Today's open local tasks (the Google ones stay with the Google import, like
  D1), each with three choices:
  - **Tomorrow:** onto tomorrow's board now (priority kept, time dropped), so tomorrow's rollover
    leaves it alone (it skips labels already on the board);
  - **Someday:** appended to `00_Inbox/Un jour.md` as `- [ ] <task> (<day>)`, never rewritten,
    read from disk first;
  - **Let it go:** remembered so tomorrow's rollover doesn't offer it.

  Today's note keeps the task as it was: nothing is rewritten.
- **The win of the day.** One field, "Today's win": a mission-log fact tagged `victoire`, so the
  evidence report gathers the wins. Work mode only.
- **The inbox count.** "N notes in the inbox", opening the inbox filing screen.
- **Close the day.** Burns today's note at once (`burnToDisk`), remembers the day as closed, and
  offers with one tap the schedule's evening mode (Personal for the owner): a manual mode until the
  schedule takes over. Never automatic; nothing to offer when that mode is already on.

## D9 — Focus sessions

- **Starting.** From Daily's + menu (25, 50 or 90 minutes; 50 by default) and from a Quick Settings
  tile that starts a 50-minute session or stops the running one. One session at a time, kept on
  the phone so it survives the app being closed. Never on a read-only device (it logs and earns
  XP).
- **The Live Update.** An ongoing notification asking for promotion (`setRequestPromotedOngoing`,
  permission `POST_PROMOTED_NOTIFICATIONS`, a normal permission: no prompt; the owner can turn Live
  Updates off in Android settings): a `ProgressStyle` bar and a countdown the system draws
  (`setUsesChronometer` counting down to the end), with Stop. Without the permission, or with Live
  Updates off, it shows as an ordinary ongoing notification. Platform APIs only (minSdk 36): no
  library change (#9).
- **Progress.** The bar steps every 5 minutes through WorkManager (one unique chain), a little
  late while the phone dozes; the countdown stays exact.
- **The end.** The last step closes the session: XP for every session that ran to its end, and a
  "Focus done · N notes edited" notification. Its "Log it" writes a mission-log fact listing the
  notes edited during the session (git history by author time, the weekly review's exclusions:
  never a private, People or generated note), in Work mode only. Stopping early ends quietly: no XP,
  no notification.

## Tests first (gate 2)

- The shutdown window: work days, 17:30 to midnight, a closed day, a day off, a schedule switched
  off.
- The task choices: tomorrow's board, the someday line appended once and read from disk first, the
  rollover leaving both alone; today's note untouched.
- Close the day: the burn first, the mode offered only when it isn't already on.
- The focus timeline: the 5-minute steps, the end, a session that outlived the app, an early stop.
- The notes of a session: the window, the exclusions; the fact's French text and its tag.
- The Live Update's builder: ongoing, promotion requested, the countdown to the end.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-11-11, interview)

1. **When the shutdown banner shows:** from 30 minutes before work ends, on work days, until the
   day is closed.
2. **Someday:** an append-only note, `00_Inbox/Un jour.md`.
3. **Close the day:** burns the note and offers the evening mode (Personal) with one tap.
4. **The win:** one line, logged as a mission-log fact tagged `victoire`, Work mode only.
5. **The Live Update permission:** added (`POST_PROMOTED_NOTIFICATIONS`).
6. **Progress:** the system's countdown and a bar stepped every 5 minutes by WorkManager; no
   foreground service.
7. **Where a session starts:** Daily's + menu and a Quick Settings tile.
8. **The end:** XP for every finished session; "Log it" adds the mission-log fact on a tap.
