## Design

### L2 — Personal experiments

- **`data/experiments/Experiments` (pure):**
  - The periods: the baseline from the start day, then the trial; the result shows the morning
    after the trial's last day, once its night is known.
  - Each outcome's daily value comes from L1's days (`HealthInsights.Day`). Mood and tasks count
    for their own day. Sleep and HRV are the night that follows the day.
  - The comparison: the baseline's measured days against the trial days the habit was ticked.
    It needs at least 7 of each.
  - The difference of the means, with a 95% percentile bootstrap: 2,000 resamples, seeded by the
    experiment's id, so a result reads the same each time.
  - "Can't tell" when the interval holds zero.
- **The file:** `10_Journal/data/experiments/experiments.json` (`ExperimentRepository`), the
  feeds pattern: disk first, an unreadable file moved aside, `VaultWriteGate`, a push requested.
- **The days:** `HealthInsightsRepository.days(first, last)`, shared with L1.
- **The card:** an Experiments card in Life Stats, after Honest insights.
  - It shows each experiment's phase and the trial's done days.
  - The result appears only once the trial is over: looking along the way invites stopping on a
    lucky day.
  - Its dialog sets the hypothesis, the habit and the outcome.
  - No New or Remove on a read-only device.

### W2 — The workshop kit

- **`data/work/Workshop` (pure):**
  - The agenda is the list under `## Ordre du jour` (or `## Agenda`).
  - The steps share the meeting's `duration_min` (60 by default) evenly, at least 5 minutes each.
  - Starting and moving to the next step keep each step's real minutes.
  - The board has four columns: ideas, decisions, actions and questions.
  - The synthesis note is French. It sits beside the meeting as "<meeting> (synthèse).md":
    - the board by column, actions as checkboxes, then the run of the agenda;
    - Nano's summary first, when asked for and when the model answers.
  - The meeting note links it under `## Atelier`.
- **`WorkshopRepository`:**
  - The draft lives on the phone (`UserPreferencesRepository.workshopDraft`), one workshop at a
    time.
  - The synthesis is written through `DraftWriter`, so it never overwrites a note.
  - The meeting note is read from disk before it is linked.
- **The screen:** route `workshop?path=`, opened from a meeting card in Meetings & requests.
  - The countdown keeps the screen on.
  - A capture field files into a column with one tap.
  - The synthesis is written in the app's scope, so leaving the screen doesn't lose it.

### W4 — The toolkit's small wins (V12 §8)

- **E7 Inbox swipe triage:**
  - A swipe to the right files the note in the suggested folder.
  - A swipe to the left snoozes it for a week. Snoozes are kept on the phone.
  - Delete is a button, with Undo.
- **E6 Board swipe and WIP limits:**
  - A swipe moves a card to the next or the previous column.
  - Each board column can have a WIP limit (kept in `boards.json`). A column over its limit
    shows the count in the error color, with a warning.
- **E1 Per-mode tabs (and widgets):** each mode hides the tabs chosen for it, set in Modes
  settings and kept in `context_modes.json`.
- **E3 A leave from the calendar:**
  - The calendar import looks ahead for all-day events named like a leave ("Congés",
    "Vacances", …).
  - Each one becomes a `LeavePeriod` marked as coming from the calendar, so a moved or deleted
    event follows. A leave typed by hand is never touched.

### X4 — Capture from anywhere

- A Quick Settings tile opens a capture screen; what is dictated or typed goes into an inbox
  note.
- An activity for `CREATE_NOTE` (the Notes role) shows the same screen over the lock screen and
  nothing else (D6).
